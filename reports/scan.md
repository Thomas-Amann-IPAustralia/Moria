## How to read these numbers

- A **trend** is significant in at least 2 of 3 windows after Holm's adjustment within its family (a source's taxonomy level or dataflow, one scope, one frequency); counts are tested as shares of the source's volume, on a log scale. Benjamini-Hochberg's count is shown only for comparison.
- An **acceleration**, an **anomaly** and a **change in level** must also be material: a change of at least 0.10 a year in growth, or at least 25% off the expected value (config/detect.yaml).
- A **weak signal** needs at least 3 items (one per de-duplication group) from at least 2 sources.
- A **flag** says the candidate would not stand without the evidence the check doubts; the candidate stays in the list, marked.
