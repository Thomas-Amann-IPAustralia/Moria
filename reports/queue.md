## How to read these numbers

- Scores are weighted geometric means of percentiles, so they rank within a scan; they are not probabilities and do not compare across scans.
- A place 'filled outside a quota' is a ring's unused place given to another ring (pass 2), or a source's cap relaxed (pass 3).
- 'Places kept' near the queue's size means the queue barely depends on that weight; a low number means the weight decides much of it, and is worth the owner's view after the first review round.
