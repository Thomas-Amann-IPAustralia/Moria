## How to read these numbers

- The anchors are synthetic sentences in `config/review.yaml`, put through the stage's own state (marked as untrusted data, as the SOP requires). A model that cannot tell an AI-inventorship ruling from a football result adds noise to a ranking.
- The spread says whether the model tells the scan's own dossiers apart at all; a question answered the same way for every dossier carries no information.
- Probabilities are uncalibrated (design §9.1); with nine anchors and twenty dossiers this is a go/no-go screen, not an accuracy measure. Accuracy needs the owner's labels (design §11.2).
