## Leakage and limits (design §11.3)

- Fitted at each date: the keyphrase vocabulary and its first-seen dates. De-duplication by URL and title only. No embeddings, clusters, micro-clusters, zero-shot tags or decision model (term-only).
- OpenAlex is left out (its topic classifier was trained in 2024); GDELT has no history before October 2026.
- Statistics take each period's latest vintage at the date, else the earliest one Moria holds (2026): revised values leak where no earlier vintage exists.
- IP RAPID is the 2026 release read at past dates: applications amended or reclassified later appear as they are now, classes by the date they were assigned where dated.
- No artefact checks: no collection history exists at past dates.
- Backfilled history has no true first sighting; non-English sources are absent.
